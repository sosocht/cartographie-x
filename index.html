20 years in 10 dates

Source: Wikipedia, "X (social network)", consulted 4 Sept. 2026 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter); CNBC 28 March 2025; NYT 2 Feb. 20263 / 15
Two eras. 2006 to 2021: Twitter invents the codes of real-time social media, the hashtag, the retweet, live reactions. Since 2022: one owner, Elon Musk, and a chain of transformations: rebrand to X, Grok AI in the feed, sale to xAI in March 2025, then the merger of SpaceX and xAI in February 2026. Today X belongs to an AI and space company, which tells you where its priorities are.
Claude

2025-2026: a platform under pressure

OwnershipxAI bought X in March 2025 ($33 bn), then SpaceX absorbed xAI in February 2026. X is now a feature of an AI group, not a standalone media company.
Regulation€120M fine from the European Commission in December 2025 under the Digital Services Act. Days later, X banned the Commission from advertising on the platform.
CompetitionIn January 2026, Threads matched X in mobile usage. Bluesky and Mastodon collect the users who left. X keeps the lead on desktop traffic.
Perception2026 SocialHub study of 500+ social media pros: X "keeps declining, in usage and in perceived importance." INSA Lyon and Université Côte d'Azur paused their accounts.
Sources: Wikipedia (4 Sept. 2026); SocialHub Mag #06 2026, p. 12, 26, 354 / 15
Four pressures at once. Ownership: X is a small part of a huge AI group. Regulation: the first big DSA fine in Europe. Competition: Threads reached parity on mobile this January. Perception: professionals rank it lower every year, and public institutions leave. Keep these four in mind, they drive our conclusion.

Who is on X? Gen Z first

The X user is young, male, and news-driven. One Gen Z user out of four is on X, four times the share of Boomers.

Worldwide, the platform claimed 550 million monthly users in September 2023, its last official figure before the xAI sale.

For a brand: X reaches opinion leaders, journalists, and early adopters, not the mass market. That is a targeting choice, not a reach choice.

Sources: Statista 2024 via course slide 52; CNBC, 18 Sept. 20236 / 15
Gen Z is the largest group, which surprises people who think X is an old network. The typical user is young, male, and there for news and opinions. So X is a targeting play: you reach the people who talk, not everybody.
Claude

When to use X (and when not to)

Use X to…
  • React to hot topics within the hour
  • Run public customer care (visible answers build trust)
  • Position expertise with structured threads
  • Live-tweet events and launches
  • Watch competitors and trends in real time
Tips that work
  • Visual first: images, video, GIFs
  • Humour and the platform's own codes
  • Relevant hashtags, not ten of them
  • No hard-sell posts: X rewards conversation
  • Answer fast: speed makes you visible
Stay away if…
  • Every post needs three levels of validation
  • Nobody can answer within the hour
  • Your brand is image-first (Instagram, TikTok fit better)
  • Your audience is mass-market, not opinion leaders
  • You cannot handle polarised replies

The algorithm rewards: relevant (past interests + engagement) · fresh (news first) · varied (no bubble) · with media (images and video boost visibility)

Sources: ECN Guide 2024 p. 8 (Conseils, Objectifs); ECN Guide 2025 p. 5 (Conseils, Algorithme)7 / 15
Simple rule: X is a conversation, not a billboard. If your organisation can answer within the hour, with humour and visuals, X works. If every post needs three signatures, you will always be late, and late is invisible on X. The algorithm confirms it: fresh and engaging content first.

Dominant sectors

NewsBreaking news, journalists, live reactions: X is still the newsroom of the internet.
PoliticsOfficials, parties, and activists. The most polarised, and the most watched, space on X.
TechFounders, developers, AI announcements (including Grok's own).
SportMatch days, transfers, clubs, and fans: live and emotional.
Brands that made it (followers)

Apple 14 M · Nike 10.1 M · Dior 9.3 M. Top French e-commerce accounts: Burger King, Les Furets, Audi France, PlayStation FR.

Who is leaving

Research labs and universities, historically strong on Twitter, moved to LinkedIn: "more possibilities", says INSA Lyon. Public institutions now weigh governance risk before posting.

Sources: ECN Guide 2025 p. 5 (Secteurs); ECN Guide 2024 p. 8 (Top 3, comptes à suivre); SocialHub Mag #06 p. 268 / 15
Four sectors own X: news, politics, tech, sport. All four share one thing: they happen live. Brands that succeed borrow that energy, like Burger King France. Sectors that need a calm, professional tone, like universities and research, have left for LinkedIn.

Square or 1.91:1, never a random crop: X crops the feed preview, and the preview decides the click.

Text on image stays readable on mobile: 62 % of the audience is on a phone screen, in a fast feed.

Media boosts reach: the algorithm explicitly favours posts with images or video.

Source: ECN Guide 2024 p. 9 (Images et profil); ECN Guide 2025 p. 5 (Algorithme)10 / 15
Four sizes to remember: 400 for the profile, 1500 by 500 for the header, 1200 square for the feed, 1200 by 675 for ads. The key idea is the preview: X crops, so we design for the crop.

Management tools: who does what

Organise & monitor

X Pro (ex-TweetDeck)

Columns, lists, several accounts, live conversation tracking. Now X Premium only.

Schedule & analyse

Hootsuite · Buffer · Social Champ

Editorial calendar, cross-platform scheduling, reports and dashboards.

Listen & protect

Brandwatch · Mention

Social listening, brand mentions, sentiment, crisis alerts and reputation.

Advertise

X for Business / X Ads

Create and run campaigns, promoted posts, polls, video ads.

Understand & retarget

Audiense Connect

Audience segmentation, measure, optimise and retarget ads.

Reality check: only 27 % of community managers say X is important for their work (ECN 2024), so most teams pick one scheduler and X Pro, not the whole stack.

Sources: ECN Guide 2024 p. 8 (Outils X); ECN Guide 2025 p. 5 (Outils)12 / 15
Five jobs, five families of tools. Organise with X Pro; schedule and analyse with Hootsuite or Buffer; listen with Brandwatch or Mention; advertise with X Ads; segment with Audiense. Most small teams need two of the five: a scheduler and X Pro.

Tools plugged into the 8-step strategy

1SMART objectives → baseline in X Analytics (impressions, engagement rate)
2Know your targets → Audiense segments, personas from real followers
3Audit & choose platforms → X Pro lists of competitors, Similarweb for traffic share
4Content plan → Hootsuite / Buffer calendar, news hooks reserved for real time
5Quality content → native video, GIFs, threads, formats from slides 10-11
6Engage → X Pro columns + Mention alerts, answer within the hour
7Analyse KPIs → impressions, engagement, clicks, conversions in Hootsuite reports
8Loyalty & e-CRM → DMs, X Communities, retargeting with Audiense and X Ads

The point: a tool is only useful at a step of the strategy. Steps 6 and 7 are where X differs from other networks: speed of reply and real-time measurement.

Two steps are specific to X: step 6, engagement, because you must answer within the hour, so you need X Pro columns and Mention alerts; and step 7, KPIs, because everything on X is measured live. The rest is the same discipline as any network.

Our position: X is no longer a default channel. It is a tactical one.

Why not by default

−11 % ad audience in France in a year, #7 network, mobile parity with Threads, and a governance risk that made universities leave.

Why still tactical

Nothing beats X for real-time: news, sport, launches, public customer care. 15.5 M intense users, one Gen Z out of four, the people who talk.

Our rule for a brand

Go on X only if you can answer within the hour. Otherwise, put the budget on Instagram or LinkedIn and keep X in watch mode.

Opening: Grok now summarises the feed for Premium users. If an AI reads X for us, does the real-time conversation that made X unique still exist, and who inherits it: Threads, Bluesky, or LinkedIn?

Reasoning built on slides 4-6 and 12-13. Claude proposed a neutral conclusion; we chose a stance. ChatGPT: counter-argument check.14 / 15
Our position, not a summary. X is not a default channel anymore: the audience shrinks, Threads caught up, the governance risk is real. But it stays tactical: for real-time, nothing else works. Our rule: go on X only if you can answer within the hour. And the opening: Grok now reads X for us. If an AI summarises the conversation, what is left of the conversation, and who inherits it?
